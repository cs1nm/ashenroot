# Shadowgrove — промты для UI v5

> Стиль утверждён: минималистичный графитовый UI с янтарными акцентами.
> Референсные изображения нужны только как концепт; финальные компоненты
> собираются в Godot из 9-slice и нарисованных кодом контролов.

## Полный HUD

```text
Brand-new minimalist UI for a 2D pixel-art sandbox adventure game, landscape
mobile screen. Crisp 16-bit pixel art, nearest-neighbor pixels, no anti-aliasing,
no gradients. Charcoal and graphite panels, cold gray one-pixel borders,
small amber accent marks, white readable icons, red health. No wood, no
parchment, no ornate fantasy frames, no large opaque HUD plates.

Classic HUD layout: compact row of ten hearts and tiny DEF/AIR/temperature
chips top-left; small time and biome at top-center; circular minimap with a
thin graphite-and-amber ring top-right; five separate floating inventory slots
bottom-center with amber corner brackets on the selected slot. Keep the center
of the game world unobstructed. Premium, restrained, high contrast, no text
placeholders, no watermark.
```

## Инвентарь

```text
Minimal charcoal pixel-art inventory UI, 16:9 landscape. Separate full screen,
dim game world behind it. Left narrow LOADOUT card with character preview and
three equipment slots. Right wide INVENTORY card with a clean 6 by 5 item grid,
selected item description and two compact action buttons. Clipped 3-pixel
corners, cold gray 1-pixel borders, tiny amber markers, white text, generous
spacing. No wood, parchment, gradients, ornament or watermark.
```

## Крафт

```text
Minimal charcoal pixel-art crafting screen, 16:9 landscape. Top station category
rail. Large scrollable recipe icon grid on the left. Focused selected-recipe
card on the right with result, station, material costs, missing ingredients and
one amber CRAFT button. Graphite panels, one-pixel gray outlines, clipped
corners, amber active state, high readability, no wood, no parchment, no
watermark.
```

## Журнал

```text
Minimal graphite pixel-art field journal screen. Horizontal category tabs on
top, compact entry index on the left, spacious detail article on the right.
Charcoal surfaces, cool gray borders, amber selection marker, off-white text,
strict pixel grid, restrained and modern, no book texture, no parchment, no
ornate frame, no watermark.
```

## Мобильные контролы

```text
Minimalist pixel-art mobile touch controls on transparent background. Fixed
movement joystick on the left, attack and jump on the right, smaller grapple
button above. Controls are thin amber rings with a second faint gray inner ring
and simple high-contrast white icons. Almost no fill, no labels, no opaque
disks, clear pressed state as a faint amber inner flash. Crisp pixels, no
anti-aliasing, no gradients, no watermark.
```
